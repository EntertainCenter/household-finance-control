Weekly Financial Check-In

Update actual bank balances and confirm income and bills that have actually cleared.

Important: The real bank is always the source of truth. Marking income Received or a bill Paid/Cleared records what happened, but does not subtract or add the money a second time. The balances entered during Check-In synchronize the system with the actual bank balances.
