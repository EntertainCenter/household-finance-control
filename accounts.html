Household Accounts

Add and maintain the real bank accounts used by Home Finance.

These are real financial accounts.

Virtual savings envelopes such as Christmas, Car Repairs or Vacation will be created separately later. They should not be entered here as bank accounts.

Edit Account

Account Role tells Home Finance what purpose the account serves.

Normally there should be one primary Bills account and one primary Spending account. Additional accounts can use Savings or Other.
Closing an account?

Mark it Inactive rather than deleting it. This allows previous transfers and activity to remain connected to the historical account.
