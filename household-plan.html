Household Plan

A concise view of what the household normally earns, what it normally costs, and where the current financial position appears to be heading.

This is the planning page — not a strict budget.

It uses your actual Home Finance data to estimate a normal household month. The goal is to show whether normal income appears sufficient, how much room is left after major commitments, how far the Bills Account is from its protected floor, and how different choices could affect the plan.

Remaining Monthly Margin —
This remaining margin is not automatically disposable spending money. It is the portion of baseline income not already assigned to recurring bills and the grocery planning assumption. From this amount must come other variable spending, rebuilding the Bills floor, extra debt reduction, savings and unexpected costs.

Grocery Planning Assumption

Groceries are kept separate from ordinary discretionary spending because they are a necessary household cost. Adjust this assumption as real experience gives you a better monthly average.

How to Read This Page

Monthly margin is positive Good starting point

Open card obligations increase Some cash is already spoken for
Debt decreases over Check-Ins Financial position improving
Major spending decision Check Forecast first
The Household Plan is intended to show direction.

It should help answer whether the household's normal income supports its normal commitments and where any remaining room could realistically be used. It is not intended to tell you that every unused dollar must immediately be assigned somewhere.
