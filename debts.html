Debt Management

Maintain credit cards, lines of credit, loans, financing and mortgages.

Balance rule: A normal positive balance means money is owed. Credit cards and store-financing accounts may also show a negative balance when the account has been prepaid. For example, -$150 means the card has a $150 credit.
Check-In is the normal place to reconcile balances.

You can correct a debt balance here when necessary, but routine weekly balance updates should be done through Check-In so the balance history is preserved consistently.
