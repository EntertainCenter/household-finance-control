Account Transfers

Move money between any active household accounts and maintain a permanent history of account activity.

Normal procedure: Create a transfer as Planned first. After you actually move the money at the bank, open the transfer and press Complete Transfer. Completing it updates both account balances and records both sides permanently in the Activity Log.
