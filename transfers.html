Transfers & Payments

Record movement between household accounts and payments made to credit cards or other debts.

Normal procedure: Create the transaction as Planned first. After you actually move the money at the bank, open it and press Complete.

An Account Transfer moves money between two bank/savings accounts. A Credit Card / Debt Payment removes money from a bank account and records that payment against the selected credit card or debt.
