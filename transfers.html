Account Transfers

Move money between any active household accounts and maintain a permanent history of account activity.

Normal procedure: Create the transfer as Planned first. When you actually move the money at the bank, open the transfer and press Complete Transfer. Completing it updates both account balances and permanently records both sides of the transaction in the Activity Log.
