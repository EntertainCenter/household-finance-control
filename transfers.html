Money Movement

Move money between accounts, manage envelopes, or make payments to debts and credit cards.

Real bank transfers should normally be created as Planned first. After the money has actually been moved at the bank, return here and choose Complete Transfer.
Envelope allocations are virtual.

If money is moved between an account and an envelope contained inside that same account, the real bank balance does not change.

Example: Savings → Christmas Envelope earmarks part of Savings without creating another bank account.
Debt payments are different from purchases.

A payment from Bills Account to a credit card reduces the bank balance and reduces the amount owed on the card. It does not create another household expense.

If the card has open household-purchase obligations, the oldest obligations are reduced automatically first.
