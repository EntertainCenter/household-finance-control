Envelopes & Savings Goals

Divide money inside your real accounts into virtual purposes without opening additional bank accounts.

Envelopes do not create additional money.

They simply identify what part of a real account you intend to reserve for a particular purpose.

Example: if Savings contains $1,000 and $300 is allocated to Christmas, the bank still contains $1,000 — Home Finance simply knows that $300 of it has a specific purpose.

Add New Envelope

Envelope balances will be changed through Transfers, not by manually editing this number.
To add money to or remove money from an envelope, use the Transfers page. That keeps a proper history of every allocation change.
